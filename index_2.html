About

Code, copy and client handoff — all in one loop.

I design and build websites for small businesses and independent brands: restaurant ordering sites, multilingual salon and service sites, and landing pages for new offers. Most of my clients speak Russian or Ukrainian, so the sites I ship are usually trilingual from day one — content, forms and SEO metadata included, not just a language switcher bolted on afterward.

I work iteratively — a working draft goes up early, and we refine it together against real feedback rather than a static mockup. Every project ends with a site the client can actually update themselves, plus a short handover so nothing depends on me being reachable forever.
